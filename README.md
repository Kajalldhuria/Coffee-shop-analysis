# Coffee-shop-analysis
# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis – Power BI

This project focuses on analyzing sales data from a coffee shop to uncover valuable business insights using **Power BI**.

---

## 📌 Objective

To visualize key sales metrics and help business stakeholders make informed decisions by identifying top-selling products, customer behavior trends, and overall performance.

---

## 📊 Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** for interactive dashboard creation
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** as the data source
- **DAX** for calculated columns and KPIs

---

## 📁 Dataset Overview

The dataset includes:
- Product names
- Sales revenue
- Quantity sold
- Transaction dates
- Customer segments
- Locations (branches/stores)

---

## 📈 Key Visuals in the Dashboard

- ✅ **Monthly Sales Trend**  
- ✅ **Top 5 Products by Sales & Quantity**  
- ✅ **Sales by Location / Branch**  
- ✅ **Category-wise Revenue Breakdown**  
- ✅ **Customer Segmentation Summary**  
- ✅ **Slicers for Dynamic Filtering**

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Product X and Y were the highest in demand during the last quarter  
- Location A outperformed others in both revenue and customer visits  
- Seasonal trends showed peak sales during weekends and holidays  
- Beverages contributed to 60% of total revenue

---

## 🧠 Skills Demonstrated

- Dashboard Design & Storytelling  
- Data Cleaning and Transformation in Power BI  
- DAX for calculated metrics  
- Slicer setup for dynamic interactivity  
- Insight communication and visual impact
